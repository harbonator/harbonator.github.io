Sam Harborne

Video Editor & Storyteller

I’ve been lucky enough to meet entrepreneurs, athletes, and creators who are living incredible lives — building businesses, traveling the world, pushing their limits. But here’s the problem: most of them never document it properly. The experiences, the energy, the lifestyle — it’s never captured and shared effectively on video.

That’s where I come in. I specialize in turning real life into cinematic stories: vlogs that don’t just show what happened, but make people feel the excitement of being there.

I’m not just an editor — I’m a storyteller. A life like yours deserves more than phone clips. It deserves a narrative.

FAQ

The questions creators ask me before we start.

How much does a video cost?

It depends on length, footage volume and turnaround. Most creators land on a monthly retainer of four long-form videos, which works out cheaper per video than one-offs. Message me with your channel and I’ll quote the same day.

How long does an edit take?

Seven days from the moment footage lands to final export, revisions included. If you need faster and the slot is free, I’ll say so honestly rather than promise it and miss.

How many revisions do I get?

Two rounds of timestamped notes. In practice most videos are signed off in the first round, because the story angle is agreed before I open a timeline.

Do you handle thumbnails and uploads?

Thumbnails, titles, chapters and scheduling are available as an add-on. I work from your channel’s retention and click-through data, not from guesswork.

Why you over a cheaper editor?

A cheap editor cuts what you shot. I build the story around it: cold open, tension, payoff, sound design. The difference shows up in average view duration, not in the export settings.

How do we start?

Send me your channel and a link to recent raw footage on WhatsApp. If it’s a fit, you get a quote and a start date within a day. If it isn’t, I’ll tell you who to speak to instead.
